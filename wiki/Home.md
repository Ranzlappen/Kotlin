## How this wiki is organized

- **Conceptual** pages (Architecture, FAQ) explain the why.
- **Operational** pages (Upgrade-History) are append-only logs.
- The **User-Manual** page mirrors the in-app manual and the Pages site —
  the three surfaces are updated together (LASTENHEFT.md rule A5).

The wiki source of truth lives in the repo's [`wiki/`](https://github.com/Ranzlappen/Kotlin/tree/main/wiki)
directory; mirror changes to the GitHub Wiki when they land.

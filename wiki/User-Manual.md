# User Manual

This is the wiki mirror of the user manual. The same content ships inside
the app (**Manual** tab, localized) and on the
[project site](https://template.ranzlappen.com/manual/). Update all three
together (LASTENHEFT.md rule A5).

## Getting started

The app opens on the Home screen. Use the navigation bar (or the rail on
large screens) to switch between Home, the manual, feedback, and settings.
Everything works offline — the app never connects to the internet on its
own.

## Theme and appearance

Under **Settings → Appearance** you can follow the system theme or force
light/dark mode. On Android 12 and newer you can also enable dynamic
color, which derives the app palette from your wallpaper.

## Language

The app ships in English and German and follows your per-app language
preference. Change it under **Settings → Language**.

## Bug reports and feature requests

Use the **Feedback** screen to report bugs or request features. Your
description plus an optional coarse device snapshot (app version, device
model, Android version — never logs or identifiers) is turned into a
report you can submit as a prefilled GitHub issue, send by email, or copy
to the clipboard.

## Privacy

The app requests no permissions, contains no trackers, and makes no
network requests. Feedback reports are only ever sent through apps you
choose (browser or email), and you always see the full content before
sending.

Manual Support Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and asks for nothing.

Data collection

  • The app requests no permissions.
  • The app makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Your settings (theme, language) are stored locally on your device only.

Feedback reports

When you use the Feedback screen, a report is composed on your device. It may include a coarse device snapshot (app version, device model, Android version, screen size, language) — never logs, never identifiers — and you can turn that off. The report leaves your device only through an app you choose (your browser or email client), and you always see the full content first.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads no third-party resources. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to the hosting.
